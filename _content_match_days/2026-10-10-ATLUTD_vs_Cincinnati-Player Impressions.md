### The Coach

{% include Player_Impression.html 
  name="Tata Martino"
  image="/images/Player_Impressions/Tata Martino 2026.webp"
  text="Atlanta began with a recognizable plan, moving the ball across the pitch and attempting to control possession.  That approach lasted roughly 15 minutes before the team began relying on individual efforts.  Cincinnati gradually took control, and Atlanta struggled to establish any sustained attacking rhythm.

  Miranchuk's introduction in the 56th minute was Martino's first adjustment, but Atlanta needed more substantial changes prior to and after that sub.  The team was visibly tiring, the midfield was struggling, and Cincinnati was finding increasing amounts of space.  Martino waited until the 86th minute to introduce Sanchez and Picault, then brought on Santos in stoppage time.  Those changes simply came far too late to make an impact."
