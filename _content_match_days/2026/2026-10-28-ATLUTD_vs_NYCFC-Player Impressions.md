## Player Impressions

Atlanta United's 2-1 defeat at Citi Field on October 28 ended the club's playoff hopes.  Atlanta controlled much of the first half, created several good scoring opportunities, and had an Embolo goal overturned for offside.  NYCFC grew into the match in the second half before Embolo equalized.  A Traoré wonder-strike in stoppage secured the win for New York City FC.
