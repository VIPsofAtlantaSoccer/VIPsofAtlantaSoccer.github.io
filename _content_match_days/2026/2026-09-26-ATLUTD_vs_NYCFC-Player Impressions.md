The central theme tonight is Atlanta's patchwork lineup.  Five players were unavailable because of international duty, and Tata Martino had to assemble a makeshift team, with several playing out of their normal positions.  Matthew Senanou, Daniel Chica, and Cameron Dunbar received short-term callups from Atlanta United 2, with Chica and Dunbar making their first-team debuts.
