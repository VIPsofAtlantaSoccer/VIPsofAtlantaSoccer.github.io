Atlanta United started Wednesday night looking ready to play.  That lasted about ten minutes.

The opening goal came from an awkward piece of work inside the Orlando box, with Paulo Díaz sending the ball across the six and Breel Embolo somehow twisting his body enough to make contact.  The goal was eventually credited as a David Brekalo own goal.

From there, Atlanta gradually lost its shape.  Orlando began finding space through midfield, getting runners behind the back line, and overloading the outside backs.  Atlanta was chasing ghosts the rest of the night.

There were individual bright spots.  Lucas Hoyos prevented the score from becoming worse.  Mauricio Amaro improved as the match went along.  Luke Brennan gave Atlanta some urgency after coming on.

There were also too many performances in which Atlanta needed considerably more.
