## Player Impressions

Atlanta United's 3-1 victory over Toronto FC provided several encouraging individual performances, particularly from Cooper Sanchez and Mauricio Amaro.  The midfield controlled large stretches of the match.  And Atlanta's attacking players created enough chances that the team could have easily had more.

The final 20 minutes also exposed some familiar weaknesses.  Toronto began finding space down Atlanta's left.  The midfield was considerably less effective after Amaro departed.
