Atlanta looked considerably more organized than it had before the World Cup break.  The defenders were encouraged to step forward, the fullbacks pushed higher, and the midfield worked hard enough to keep Nashville uncomfortable for long stretches. The early injury to Jay Fortune disrupted Atlanta’s rhythm, and the team eventually began to tire in the second half.  Atlanta still remained in the match until the final whistle, but another night of promising possession and poor execution ended without a goal.
