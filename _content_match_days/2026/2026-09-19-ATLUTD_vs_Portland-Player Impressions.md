Atlanta United left Providence Park with a 1-0 win over the Portland Timbers.  Alexey Miranchuk supplied the lone goal and the entire team protected it.  Atlanta played some of its most fluid soccer of the season during the first half, then relied on committed defending in the second.  Several players delivered their best performances in an Atlanta shirt. Others contributed in important ways while still leaving Atlanta needing more.
