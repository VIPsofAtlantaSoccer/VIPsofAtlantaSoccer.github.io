### Midfield 

{% include Player_Impression.html
    name="Adrian Gill"
    image="/images/Player_Impressions/Adrian Gill 2026.webp"

He repeatedly stepped into passing lanes, broke up attacks and moved the ball forward with confidence.  By the second half he was trying flicks and fakes, and his late block helped protect the lead.  Atlanta's best midfielder on the night."
%}

{% include Player_Impression.html
    name="Cooper Sanchez"
    image="/images/Player_Impressions/Cooper Sanchez 2026.webp"
    text="Recovered from a difficult opening and grew into the midfield.  He worked hard defensively, made several good forward passes and helped Atlanta find its way through New York's press.  There is still another level needed from him, but the passing and defensive work were useful throughout his night."
%}

{% include Player_Impression.html
    name="Tristan Muyumba"
    image="/images/Player_Impressions/Tristan Muyumba 2026.webp"
    text="A frustrating performance turned into a decisive one.  He wasted Atlanta's best chance of the first half when he sent an open shot over the bar, then answered with a wonderful curling finish into the top corner after halftime.  The goal changed him.  He became more aggressive on the ball, carried it forward and looked like he wanted another.  When Muyumba raises his tempo, he can completely change a game."
%}

### The Front Line

{% include Player_Impression.html
    name="Miguel Almirón"
    image="/images/Player_Impressions/Miguel Almirón.jpg"
    text="He is Atlanta's heart and soul.  He ran constantly, carried the ball out of pressure and kept trying to create something even when the attack around him was struggling.  His long pass created Muyumba's first-half chance, and his cross set up Picault's winner.  Even after coming off, he remained on the sideline directing traffic and urging the team through the final minutes."
%}

{% include Player_Impression.html

    image="/images/Player_Impressions/Alexey Miranchuk.jpg"

Even so, he was involved in both goals, flicking the ball to Muyumba for the equalizer and combining with Almirón before Picault's winner."

{% include Player_Impression.html

%}
