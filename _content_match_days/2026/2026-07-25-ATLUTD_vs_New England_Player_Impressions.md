Atlanta United had talented players working hard, but it did not have a functioning team.

Miguel Almirón and Alexey Miranchuk moved across the field trying to create openings. Tristan Muyumba occasionally broke pressure with an ambitious pass. Matt Edwards delivered balls from the right. Fafà Picault pressed New England’s back line despite playing outside his natural position.

The individual effort could not hide the structural failures around them.  Atlanta’s midfield was outclassed, its defensive shape was repeatedly pulled apart and its attackers kept sending balls into spaces where no teammate was making a run.  Atlanta largely searched for an individual moment that might provide something, anything.

One eventually came through Almirón, Miranchuk and Cayman Togashi. By then, New England had scored four.
