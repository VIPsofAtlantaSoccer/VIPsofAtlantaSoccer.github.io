Atlanta United finished its 2026 season with a 3-0 defeat to the New York Red Bulls on Saturday.  Jayden Hibbert received his first league start of the season, but Tata Martino otherwise relied on his regular starters.  The Red Bulls controlled long stretches of the match, repeatedly finding space through Atlanta's midfield and creating chances against a struggling back line.

Atlanta had occasional opportunities, including Miguel Almirón's shot off the post early in the first half, but struggled to maintain possession or involve its forwards.  The second half offered little improvement, leaving Martino with another poor performance from a lineup that has struggled throughout the season.
