## Player Impressions

Atlanta United's 3-1 loss to Inter Miami was a match of two very different halves.  Atlanta looked organized and competitive through much of the first half, with several players making strong contributions.  Miami adjusted after halftime, and Atlanta struggled to respond.  Those differences were particularly noticeable in the midfield and along the back line.
