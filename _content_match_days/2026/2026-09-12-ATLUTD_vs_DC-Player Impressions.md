Atlanta United was the better team during its scoreless draw with D.C. United at Audi Field. The first half moved slowly, but Atlanta began finding combinations and creating better opportunities after halftime.

The final touch repeatedly let Atlanta down. Miguel Almirón missed several chances, Breel Embolo struggled to remain involved near goal, and Luke Brennan came within inches of winning the match during stoppage.
