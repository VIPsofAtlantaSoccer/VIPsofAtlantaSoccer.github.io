{% include Player_Impression.html 
  name="Tomás Jacob"
  image="/images/Player_Impressions/Tomás Jacob 2026.webp"

His willingness to keep joining the attack gave Atlanta an unexpected source of production from right back."

Gill protected the ball under pressure, stayed on his feet and found a teammate in space rather than settling for a clearance.
