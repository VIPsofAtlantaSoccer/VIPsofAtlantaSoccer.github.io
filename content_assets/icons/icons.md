
# icons


## overall style:
- Clean flat vector icons
- Transparent background
- Square canvas
- Object fills 75% to 90% of the canvas
- Simple geometric shapes
- Moderate rounded corners
- Minimal detail
- Limited shading
- No [icon-icons](heavy sticker outline
- No text
- No drop shadow
- No background badge unless specifically requested
- Readable at 24px to 32px

## Chosen Style prompt
```
Create a transparent-background website icon for: [whistle].

Style: playful illustrated soccer icon, polished vector-style rendering, bold shapes, smooth curves, slightly exaggerated proportions, fun and energetic sports-branding aesthetic.  The main subject should feel stylized and visually engaging rather than plain or generic.  Square canvas.  Main subject fills most of the canvas with modest padding.  Transparent background.  No text.

A soccer ball should not be included in an object which normally doesn't have a soccer ball, ex: a key.

Color direction: use an expanded sports-branding palette with charcoal black as the anchor color, muted gold as a premium accent, field green for pitch and field-related elements, cyan as a bright modern accent, white for highlights and markings, and neutral grays for secondary surfaces. Use subject-appropriate colors where needed, such as green for a soccer pitch, red for a red card, and yellow for a yellow card.

Use a flat pure magenta background color #FF00FF.  Do not use checkerboard, gradient, texture, shadow, or background pattern.  Keep the icon fully separated from the background.
```
