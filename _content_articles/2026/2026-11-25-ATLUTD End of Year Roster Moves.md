Atlanta United announced its year-end roster moves on Wednesday.  As of now, 27 players are heading into the 2027 Sprint season.

---
## Who Leaves

All of the players leaving are attackers.  Atlanta declined options on Picault, Santos, and Ashton Gordon.  Cayman Togashi is out of contract at the end of the year.

Picault played in 24 matches, 15 of them off the bench.  Santos played in 10, all off the bench.  Togashi in eight.

---
## Who Stays

The club exercised options through December 31, 2027 on Matt Edwards, Jayden Hibbert, Tristan Muyumba, Santiago Pita, Will Reilly, and Cooper Sanchez.  The end date is important as MLS moves to a European calendar next season.  The 2027-28 season starts in the summer.  These six are set to run out of contract in the middle of it.

Atlanta exercised the option on Lucas Hoyos through the 2027 Sprint season.

Kaiden Moore and Adyn Torres return from loan on January 1.  Moore spent the season with the Philadelphia Union.  Torres was with FC Nõmme United in Estonia.

Galoppo stays on loan from River Plate through the Sprint Season.  Latte Lath stays on loan at Union Berlin through the completion of the 2026-27 season.  

Atlanta has options on both contracts.

---
## The Roster

This is not the final roster of course.  The SuperDraft, free agency, trades, and transfers can still bring in new players or move them out before the Sprint season.

The 2026 numbers cover all 36 matches: 34 in MLS and two in the Open Cup.

### Goalkeepers (4)

Hoyos started 33 of 36 matches Atlanta played this year.  However, his contract runs only through the Sprint season, not the full 2027 calendar year.

Behind him, Atlanta has three keepers.  Hibbert started three matches, two of them in the Open Cup.  Donaldson and Ransom become Homegrown Players on January 1.  Ransom started 14 matches for Atlanta United 2 this season.  Donaldson started seven.

The Sprint season looks like a handoff from Hoyos.  When the 2027-28 season starts, someone new will likely be in goal.

We cannot assume Hibbert will take over.  His contract now runs only through December 31, 2027, the middle of the new season.  He could still sign a new deal.  But it may be a sign that he is not the club's next No. 1.  It could be one of the two Homegrowns, or a keeper Atlanta has not signed yet.

### Forwards (3)

The front line is light on options, with only Embolo and Brennan the only recognized strikers.

Latte Lath is on loan at Union Berlin through the end of the 2026-27 season, and is widely assumed to be a buy-out candidate.  

The upcoming No. 4 draft pick and free agency are the first chances Atlanta will have to add depth to the striker pool.
