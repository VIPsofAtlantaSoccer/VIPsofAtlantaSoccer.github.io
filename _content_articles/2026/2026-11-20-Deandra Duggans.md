Atlanta City FC has removed Deandra Duggans, the club's Vice President and Chief Business Officer, from its official front office directory.  Her individual biography has also disappeared from the website, returning a 404 error.  The changes were discovered Thursday, November 19, raising questions about her future with the organization less than seven months after her appointment.

Duggans joined Atlanta City in April 2026, taking responsibility for the club's business operations, including marketing, sponsorships, ticket sales, digital strategy, and community engagement.  She reported directly to Mauricio Culebro, AMB Sports and Entertainment's President of Soccer, and was among the senior executives tasked with building the organization ahead of its inaugural NWSL season in 2028.

As recently as November 2, Duggans was publicly representing the club during the introductory press conference for Chief Soccer Officer Richard Gunney.  She spoke with WSB-TV about Atlanta City's growing support.

Duggans was also noticeably absent from Atlanta City's November 18 announcement naming [Wellstar Health System as the naming rights partner for the club's training facility](/2026/11/18/atlcity-training-ground).  The agreement represents a major commercial partnership for the organization, an area that falls within the responsibilities of the Chief Business Officer.  The announcement included statements from Mauricio Culebro, AMBSE President Tim Zulawski, and Arthur Blank, but made no mention of Duggans.

Information provided to VIPs of Atlanta Soccer indicates that Duggans was still communicating on behalf of the club Monday morning, November 16.  Later that afternoon, the same individual reportedly learned that Duggans was no longer employed by Atlanta City FC.  VIPs has not independently confirmed her departure.

Neither the club nor Duggans has publicly announced a departure.  It remains unclear whether she has left the organization, changed positions, or whether the website changes reflect another internal development.

VIPs of Atlanta Soccer has contacted AMB Sports and Entertainment for clarification regarding Duggans' status and is awaiting a response.

With Atlanta City continuing to build its business operations ahead of its 2028 debut, the reported departure of one of its senior executives would represent a significant leadership change less than two years before the club takes the field.
