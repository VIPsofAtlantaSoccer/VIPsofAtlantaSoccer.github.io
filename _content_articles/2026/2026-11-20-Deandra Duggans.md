Deandra Duggans is no longer with Atlanta City FC, AMB Sports and Entertainment confirmed Friday.  The club's Vice President and Chief Business Officer departs less than seven months after joining the organization.  Her departure comes as Atlanta City continues building its business operations ahead of its inaugural NWSL season in 2028.

Duggans joined Atlanta City in April 2026, taking responsibility for the club's business operations, including marketing, sponsorships, ticket sales, digital strategy, and community engagement.  She reported directly to Mauricio Culebro, AMB Sports and Entertainment's President of Soccer, and was among the senior executives tasked with building the organization ahead of its inaugural NWSL season in 2028.

As recently as November 2, Duggans was publicly representing the club during the introductory press conference for Chief Soccer Officer Richard Gunney.  She spoke with WSB-TV about Atlanta City's growing support.

Duggans was also noticeably absent from Atlanta City's November 18 announcement naming [Wellstar Health System as the naming rights partner for the club's training facility](/2026/11/18/atlcity-training-ground).  The agreement represents a major commercial partnership for the organization, an area that falls within the responsibilities of the Chief Business Officer.  The announcement included statements from Mauricio Culebro, AMBSE President Tim Zulawski, and Arthur Blank, but made no mention of Duggans.

Information provided to VIPs of Atlanta Soccer indicates that Duggans was still communicating on behalf of the club Monday morning, November 16.  Later that afternoon, the same individual reportedly learned that she was no longer employed by Atlanta City FC.  AMBSE has since confirmed her departure, although the organization has not specified when she left.

AMB Sports and Entertainment confirmed to VIPs of Atlanta Soccer that Duggans is no longer with the organization.  The circumstances surrounding her departure remain unclear.  AMBSE has not provided a reason for her exit.

Duggans' departure leaves Atlanta City without one of the senior executives responsible for building its commercial operations ahead of the club's 2028 debut.  With less than two years before the team takes the field, the organization faces another leadership decision as preparations for its inaugural season continue.
