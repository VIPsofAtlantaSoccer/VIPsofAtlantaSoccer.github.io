Tristan Muyumba was out on the training pitch Tuesday morning.  His contract expires Dec. 31, and the club has not said whether it will pick up his option.  Inside, the people who will make that call sat down to explain a second straight season without playoff soccer.

Mauricio Culebro looked at ease.  Chris Henderson did not.

Culebro announced a reorganization of the sporting department.  Then Doug Roberson of *SoccerByDoug* asked the first question included, "To be blunt, why was Chris not also held accountable for this season?"  Henderson stared him down.

When it was over, Culebro walked around the room shaking hands.  Henderson left.

---
## Four Hires, One Chain of Command

Culebro opened by calling the season "unacceptable".  Missing the postseason two years running, he said, "is not meeting our expectations for this team."  After evaluating the whole operation over the past few months, he is changing the process in three places: **roster building**, **player recruitment**, and **decision-making**.  The reorganization began in earnest last week when Efstathiou and Spector were let go.

Atlanta will be hiring four people, all of whom will report to Henderson.  Culebro called three of the jobs new.  Going by what he described, only two are.  One replaces Jonathan Spector.  The other is a narrower version of what Dimitrios Efstathiou did.  Culebro said the club needs to remember it's a soccer team, and put its resources where they matter most.

| Position | New? | What Culebro described |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical Director | Yes | Oversees performance, medical, and physical.  Links the academy, the 2's, and the first team.  Supports Henderson day to day. |
| Assistant Sporting Director | Yes | Additional support for the sporting operation. |
| Cap and Contracts | Partly | A more specific version of the work Dimitrios Efstathiou was doing. |
| Head of Scouting and Analysis | No | Replaces Jonathan Spector.  Same role, but possibly a different profile and slightly different responsibilities. |

The technical director is the most interesting of the four.  This person will work closely with Henderson and can push back on him.  Culebro doesn't want Henderson to be the "*one mind*" on the sporting side.  The technical director will also cover the day-to-day when Henderson is out scouting.  The role is meant to connect the different Atlanta United teams, from the academy to the first team.

Culebro said the academy has grown in importance, and pointed to how many first-team players came through it.  He also said it's hard for young players to break in when the team isn't performing well.  They need a better environment to keep developing.

Roberson asked whether the replacement jobs were a step down.  Culebro said the new version of Efstathiou's job would be more specific, and would now report to Henderson.  He called that new organization an important distinction.  What Efstathiou actually handled was never entirely clear from the outside.

Culebro's goal is to take weight off Henderson.  He said the club wants to free Henderson from some of his responsibilities, so he can focus on what really adds value.  He wants it to be clear who is involved in each signing and who makes the final decision.  For the new hires, he wants people with experience, who fit the culture, and who understand what the club is going through.

---
## Not About Blame

Roberson also asked what Efstathiou and Spector weren't doing that the reorganization will fix.  Culebro started by thanking both men.  He said the changes aren't about putting the blame on anyone.

Culebro defended Henderson's record, saying Henderson has won titles everywhere he's been.  He said the club needs continuity after years of turnover.  He also pointed to his own timing.  Culebro arrived in the middle of last summer's transfer window, during an unusual World Cup year.

When asked about Tata Martino, Culebro admitted the numbers are clear, and the season wasn't acceptable.  But he said they looked at more than the numbers.  He pointed to Martino's experience and his winning record in MLS, with titles in Atlanta and Miami.  The season also had a lot of turnover in the middle of the year.  The club wants continuity, and Culebro wants Henderson and Martino to lead the project into 2027.

Jose Prosopio of *latingoles* asked how Atlanta's situation compares to his previous jobs.  Culebro said he's been through bad stretches before, at Tigres and Club América, even though both clubs regularly compete for titles.  He said different leagues, rules, and situations make it hard to compare, but that his experience shaped the changes he's making now.

## Building the Roster

Sam Jones of *Five Stripe Final* asked Henderson if it was a mistake that Martino had a lot of influence on roster decisions this season, and how much influence Martino will have going forward.

Henderson said collaboration with the coach is part of every signing.  The club doesn't want to sign a player the coach doesn't want to coach.  He said the ideal is to narrow it to several candidates everyone likes, so the club is happy with whichever one it can sign.  The new structure, he said, will make the decision process clearer.

Culebro said he will stay involved in recruitment.  He admitted he isn't a soccer expert and never played, but said he has ideas and experience.  He sees his job as adding value where he can, working with Henderson, Martino, and the scouting department.

Asked where the team fell short, Henderson pointed to both boxes.  He said Atlanta was sometimes very good in possession and through midfield, but lacked precision creating chances and breaking down defenses.  The same was true defending its own box, and he said some games tilted against Atlanta because of it.  He wants players who can beat someone one-on-one and deliver service, and he wants to find ways to set up Breel Embolo and play to his strength.

Henderson also expects more from the players who arrived during the season.  He said Embolo came straight off a long season, and now has a real sense of the league and where he can make an impact.  He thinks the other summer signings will come in better prepared, too.  About seven or eight players didn't go through a full preseason with Atlanta.  Henderson said this one will be important for building fitness, relationships, a tactical identity, and a stronger mentality.

---
## The Sprint Season

Martino has said next season would bring only small changes.  Henderson expects more than that.  He said the club can reinforce the group through trades and other moves inside and outside MLS.  Those talks with Martino are ongoing, and the new director of recruitment will join them once hired.  Henderson wants to stay flexible, and may look for different types of players at some positions.

Martino also said most of the roster is coming back, then said a couple of weeks later that the team needs wingers.  Roberson asked what that means for Miguel Almirón and Alexey Miranchuk.  Both are designated players, and Roberson said neither has produced at a DP level over the past two seasons.  Henderson said the plan is to bring players back, but talks with other clubs and with the players themselves are ongoing.  He pointed out that both can play several positions.  He also defended Almirón.

Miranchuk got no such endorsement.

Atlanta left both of its buyouts unused this season.  Henderson said they're one option the club will look at closely, and that ownership has supported the club throughout.

Asked how the club scouts a player's mentality, Henderson said it starts with live scouting.  He said video doesn't show how a player reacts off the ball, or how he deals with teammates and coaches.  Watching a player train helps when that's possible, he said, and so do one-on-one interviews about why a player wants to come to MLS.  Asked whether that wasn't happening enough, Henderson said the process was good, but it was hard for him to get out and see players this year.

Henderson said the Sprint Season means the team has to be ready from day one.  He wants Mercedes-Benz Stadium to become a real home-field advantage.  He stressed that the Sprint Season counts as a full season, with an MLS Cup on the line.

The press conference had plenty of detail on structure and few specifics on players.  Henderson said the new alignment has everyone moving in the same direction.  Culebro closed with a message to fans, first in Spanish, then in English.
