Atlanta City FC's training ground has a name.  AMBSE and Wellstar Health System announced Wednesday that the club's future home in Marietta will be called Wellstar Performance Center.

In addition to the naming rights, Wellstar will be the club's official healthcare provider and will serve as the training gear partner.  AMBSE believes it is among the most comprehensive training facility naming deals in NWSL history.  AMBSE did not announce the value or length of the deal.

At his [introductory press conference on November 2](/2026/11/02/richard-gunney/), Chief Soccer Officer Richard Gunney said Atlanta City could start signing players in January.  

---
## Westar

Wellstar is one of the largest nonprofit health systems in Georgia, with 13 hospitals and about 35,000 employees.  Mauricio Culebro said Wellstar will lend its expertise to make sure Atlanta City's players receive the best care.

During match days at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Wellstar will be known as the presenting sponsor and their name will be on digital signage.

The club and Wellstar will jointly run a charity program.  In addition, they will host community events centered around women's health and orthopedics at the training center once a year.

AMBSE President Tim Zulawski said Wellstar was a natural fit as an Atlanta-based healthcare company focused on women's health.  Wellstar CEO Ketul J. Patel said the deal lets Wellstar reach more women and families across the region.

---
## The Facility

The nearly 33-acre site at 1033 Franklin Gateway will have four full fields and two half-pitches.

The facility will have space for a second team and other developmental teams.  The [plans filed with Marietta this summer](/2026/08/21/nwsl-training-ground/) showed two artificial-turf fields that can each hold two U12 fields, and an area set aside for future development.

**Populous and Impact** are handling the design and development of the Wellstar Performance Center.

Atlanta City, the City of Marietta, and NWSL Commissioner Jessica Berman [broke ground on the site in April](/2026/07/09/nwsl-ground/).  The work is still in the site-prep stage.  Crews are leveling the ground and laying pipe, ahead of any building going up.  The club's goal is to open the facility before its first season in 2028.

## The Soccer Corridor

Atlanta United's training ground is roughly a 10-minute walk up Franklin Gateway.  With both the Atlanta United and Atlanta City training grounds, plus related infrastructure, AMBSE will have invested about $200 million along the corridor.

Arthur Blank thanked Wellstar CEO Ketul Patel and AMBSE's leadership for putting the deal together.
