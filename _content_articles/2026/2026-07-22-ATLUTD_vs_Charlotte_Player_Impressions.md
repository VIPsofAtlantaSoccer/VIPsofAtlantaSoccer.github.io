---

Atlanta United's first half was disorganized, disconnected and the Five Stripes were fortunate to be only two goals down. Charlotte found space too easily, Atlanta struggled to play through even modest pressure, and the back line never looked settled.  The second half was considerably better.  Atlanta played with more urgency, Báez and Almirón pulled the team level, and Lucas Hoyos repeatedly kept Charlotte from re-taking the lead.  A comeback draw was an unlikely result considering how poorly Atlanta played for much of the night.  The response after halftime showed considerably more fight than the opening 45 minutes.
