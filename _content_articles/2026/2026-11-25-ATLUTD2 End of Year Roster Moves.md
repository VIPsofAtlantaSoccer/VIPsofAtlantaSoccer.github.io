Atlanta United 2 announced its end-of-season roster moves on Wednesday.  Five players are under contract for the 2027 Sprint season.  Three more are still negotiating.

Five.  The locker room is close to empty.  

---
## The Changes

In goal, James Donaldson and Jonathan Ransom join the first team as Homegrown Players on January 1.  One, or both, could be loaned back to ATLUTD2 next season.

Mohamed Cisset and Matthew Senanou started almost every match at center back.  Both signed in January.  The club declined both of their options.

Toto Majub stays.  He started seven matches.  The club exercised his option through December 31, 2027, in the middle of the 2027-28 season, the same as the six first-team options.

Daniel Chica, the regular right back, re-signed for the Sprint season.  The club has an option to keep him for 27/28.

Andrew Jardines and D.D. Sibrian are both under contract through the Sprint season.  Jardines started 15 matches at defensive midfield.  Sibrian started 10.  Jardines signed his contract in August.  It runs through the 27/28 MLS NEXT Pro season.

Cameron Dunbar, Enzo Dovlo, and Ignacio Suarez-Couri started most matches in the attacking midfield.  All three are out of contract and negotiating with the club.  All three played for the first team this season.

Arif Kovac is out of contract.  He won the MLS NEXT Pro Golden Boot with 20 goals.  Reports [linked him with Kalmar FF in Sweden](/2026/10/13/arif-kovac-to-sweden/).  Atlanta offered him a new deal.  But I think he is gone.

The club declined options on Liam Butts and Patrick Weah.  Both forwards came off the bench most of the season.

Caio Assis is the fifth player under contract.  He made one appearance, off the bench.
